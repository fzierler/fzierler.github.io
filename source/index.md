Hi, I am a particle physics post-doc at the Technical University of Munich, Germany. I work in the [research group of Prof. Nora Brambilla T30F.](https://einrichtungen.ph.nat.tum.de/T30f/)

My current research interests include:

- Lattice Field Theory
- Strongly Interacting Gauge Theories
- Exotics and XYZ States
- Composite Dark Matter
- Hadron Spectroscopy and Scattering
- Finite Temperature Phase Transitions
- Dyson-Schwinger/Bethe-Salpeter Equations
